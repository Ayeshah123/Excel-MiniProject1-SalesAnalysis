# 📊 Exploring Sales Data | Excel

Rather than looking at sales data as a collection of transactions, I approached it through a set of business questions:

- Where is revenue coming from?
- Which products are most profitable?
- How does sales activity differ across channels?

---

## 📌 Overview

This mini project explores sales data using Microsoft Excel. The analysis covers revenue, profitability, products, countries, order priorities, and sales channels.

---

# 🔍 Analysis

## 🌍 Country Revenue Analysis

### Analysis Focus
- Revenue by country
- Top 10 countries by revenue

### Key Findings

- Portugal generated the highest revenue at **£9.83M**, followed by Poland at **£9.59M** and Bulgaria at **£8.70M**.
- The top 10 countries generated between **£4.63M and £9.83M** in revenue.

---

## 📦 Product Profit Analysis

### Analysis Focus
- Total profit by product
- Comparison of product profitability

### Key Findings

- Office Supplies generated the highest total profit at **£9.53M**, followed by Vegetables at **£5.58M** and Meat at **£4.50M**.
- Household generated the lowest total profit at **£2.14M**.

---

## 🚚 Order Priority Analysis

### Analysis Focus
- Orders by product and order priority

### Key Findings

- Personal Care had the highest number of Medium-priority orders (**8**) and Low-priority orders (**7**).
- Office Supplies had the highest number of High-priority orders (**7**), while Household had **0 High-priority orders**.

---

## 🛒 Sales Channel Analysis

### Analysis Focus
- Orders by product and sales channel
- Comparison of Online and Offline sales

### Key Findings

- Personal Care had **11 Online** and **12 Offline** orders, giving it the highest total order volume (**23**).
- Household had the lowest order volume, with **2 Online** and **5 Offline** orders (**7 total**).
- Snacks had more Online orders (**11**) than Offline orders (**6**), while Personal Care had more Offline orders (**12**) than Online orders (**11**).
